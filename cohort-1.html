Cohort 1 (Pilot)

Cohort 1 is a small, time-boxed pilot to validate the format, matching quality, and participant experience before scaling. The goal is consistency and learning, not volume.

Start window: Week of Jan 6, 2026
Session format: 20–30 minute 1:1 sessions
Mentor commitment: 1–2 sessions in January (best-effort)
Mentee expectation: show up prepared with 1–3 focused questions

Scope

  • Mentors: targeted initial group (quality over quantity)
  • Mentees: mid-career (typically 5+ years experience)
  • Topics: system design, backend/distributed systems, interview strategy, resume feedback, career transitions, leadership navigation
  • Not included: referrals or job placement (mentorship only)

Timeline and deadlines

  1. Mentor intake: Dec 18–Dec 29
  2. Mentor list locked: Dec 30
  3. Mentee applications open: Jan 2–Jan 5
  4. Matching + intros: Jan 6–Jan 7
  5. Sessions begin: Week of Jan 6
  6. End-of-cohort feedback: Jan 27–Jan 31

How matching works in Cohort 1

  1. Mentees apply with a short background + what they want help with.
  2. I review requests for clarity and fit (to protect mentor time).
  3. I manually match based on topic fit, time zone overlap, and availability.
  4. Scheduling happens through the mentor’s preferred method (Calendly if available).

What mentors can expect

  • Short sessions. No prep required.
  • A short written background before each session (2–5 minutes to skim).
  • Control over calendar and boundaries. You can pause at any time.

What mentees can expect

  • Honest, practical guidance based on the mentor’s experience.
  • High-signal conversations if you come prepared with specific questions.
  • No guarantees of ongoing support beyond the session.
