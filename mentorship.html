Diaspora Mentorship Network

A volunteer initiative connecting experienced diaspora engineers with mid-career developers (5+ years experience) for focused, 20–30 minute 1:1 mentorship sessions.

Join as a Mentor

Volunteer a small number of sessions per month and help engineers who are aiming to grow into senior and staff-level roles.

Mentor signup form

Apply as a Mentee

For engineers with 5+ years of experience who want guidance on system design, interviews, career decisions, or technical leadership.

Mentee signup form

How Matching Works

1. Mentees apply with their background, topic, and availability.
2. We manually review each request and match to a mentor whose skills align.
3. Sessions are scheduled via the mentor’s Calendly link, respecting their time and boundaries.
4. Each session is a focused, 20–30 minute conversation on a specific topic or question.
