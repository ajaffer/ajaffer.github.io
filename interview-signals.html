How it works

  • Typed, not prose: every few seconds a rolling window of transcript becomes a phase choice plus bounded scores for answered-question, depth, clarity, and rambling risk. Values, not paragraphs, so thresholds and rate limits are just code.
  • Suppression is the product: 111 of 119 judgments in this session were discarded — below threshold, unchanged, low-confidence, or too soon after the last card. A correct signal can still be bad product behavior.
  • Some questions never reach the model: preconditions in code — no open question, too little candidate speech, a turn too short to ramble — blocked 81 of 200 questions in this session, 40% of them, before a call was made. Duration and counting stay in code too, because Jev 1.13 does not count reliably; TypeSafe's own guidance is that extraction is a judgment and arithmetic is not.
  • Cost: a quarter of a cent for the whole 10-minute interview — 39 calls, 60k input tokens, at Jev's $0.042 per million input tokens with output free. About 1.5¢ per interview-hour, at a median 119 ms per call, which is what makes a per-tick cadence viable at all.
